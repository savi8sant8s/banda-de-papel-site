FAQ

Rules
  • Payment before I start. The up-to-10-business-days delivery counts from payment. In a hurry? Ask in your request.
  • A preview to check. I fix my own mistakes for free (lyrics, captions out of sync, misspelled name). Changes of mind after the preview are charged separately.
  • Cut-paper style. The figures are inspired by the people in the video (hair, clothes, instrument). They're not caricatures or photos.
  • Music rights. Gift videos are for personal use, not monetized. To promote, you confirm you own the rights to the song or have permission to use it.
  • Portfolio. I may show the video on the channel unless you ask me not to.
  • Refunds. If I can't deliver, you get 100% back. No refunds after the preview is sent.
Can I pick any song?

Yes, as long as it has vocals and lyrics I can caption. Instrumentals work too, just without captions.

Can you do the whole song?

Videos go up to 60 seconds, the Reels, Shorts and TikTok format. If you need more, tell me in your request.
