How it works (and the rules)

I make these videos in my spare time, so the rules settle everything before I start.

  1. An order isn't a purchase. Send your order through the form and I'll reply within 3 business days saying whether I can take it. If I'm fully booked, I'll tell you and you pay nothing.
  2. Payment before I start. If I accept, I'll email you a card payment link. The deadline starts when the payment clears.
  3. Delivery: up to 10 business days after payment (or 3 business days with rush delivery).
  4. Everything is set in the order. Song, part (start and end), scene and kick drum text. Once I start, these don't change.
  5. One preview to check. I fix my own mistakes for free: wrong lyrics, out-of-sync captions, misspelled names. A change of mind is charged as an extra.
  6. It's the paper band style. The characters are the band's own; I don't do caricatures or real people's faces.
  7. Music rights. Gift videos are for personal use, not monetized. For Artist, you confirm you own the song or have permission to use it.
  8. Portfolio. I may show the video on the channel and on this site, unless you ask me not to.
  9. Refunds. If I can't deliver, you get 100% back. Once the preview is sent, there are no refunds.

FAQ

Can I pick any song?

Yes, as long as it has vocals and lyrics that can be captioned. Instrumentals work too, just without captions.

Can you do the whole song?

Packages go up to 60 seconds, the Reels, Shorts and TikTok format. If you need more, ask in your order.

Can you put someone's face on the characters?

No. The characters are always the paper band. You can customize the kick drum text.

Can I post my gift video on social media?

Yes, on your personal profile. Since the song belongs to someone else, it can't be monetized.
